## Tutorial

### Learn the basics

Shows a tutorial with options to SKIP or Continue on each topic

> Categorize your Expenses and Income records

#### Category

- Categories are labels for organizing expenses. FinanceManager is preloaded with common 
income and expense categories, but you can add more as you need.

> Manage your Money Sources

#### Account

- An account represents a source of money, like wallets, cards, etc. Start with the
included accounts or add new ones.

#### Record

- One record for every expense or income activity. Take records while paying for something.
Add your earning records as well.
